Is a problem related to GNNs and not transformers. In a GNN the information flows through edges to the neighbour nodes. After $L$ layers, the information can be captured only to a distance $\leq L$. If the graph is large, distant information can be *squashed* into a constant dimension vector. A fully-connected transformer overcome this problem because every input token (nodes or edges) has a direct connection to every other node in the sequence.
